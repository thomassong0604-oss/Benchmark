Definitions and sources

“Relieved” means sentenced without the mandatory minimum applying, through the safety valve, substantial assistance to the government, or both. Shares of drug cases are of FY2024 federal drug cases. The “Other” race share for crack cocaine is not reported separately. Criminal history, which drives safety-valve eligibility, differs across drugs, and so do statutory penalties, so nothing here shows that any individual was treated differently.
